Work done by forces

Learning objective: Describe the energy transfers that take place when work is done on an object

Define force.

Force is a push or pull.

State the SI unit for force.

newtons (N)

State the different stores of energy.

Kinetic, chemical, gravitational potential, elastic potential, nuclear and thermal

Describe the energy transfers that take place when a skateboarder comes down a ramp.

The gravitational potential store is emptied and the kinetic store is filled. Energy is also transferred to the thermal store of the wheels and the ramp due to friction.
